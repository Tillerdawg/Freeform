# Derived-coordinate-first dot entry

Status: accepted implementation decision for Kanban task `t_572aa154`.

DECISION | Wheeljack | Dot placement grammar, snapping default, and future performer-instruction parity | Make derived NFHS coordinate entry the primary dot-placement path. Its data model uses exact integer quarter-step counts and builds canonical integer FU before issuing the existing document command. Keep raw FU inside a clearly secondary Advanced control. Provide a reusable nearest-quarter-step snap utility whose landmark/line selection and half-away-from-zero ties match coordinate inspection. Treat this grammar as the future performer-packet instruction representation, not a display-only convenience. | Drill writers author in sides, yard lines, landmarks, and directions; canonical FU remains necessary for storage, distance, and motion math. Whole quarter steps are exact 450-FU increments, avoiding float-induced coordinate changes. Raw FU remains necessary for intentional off-grid reproduction and debugging. Shared snapping prevents future field interaction from introducing a second rounding convention. | The structured form dispatches only `dot.create` and `dot.move`, so command history remains the sole mutation boundary. Inspection strings that were produced by rounding an off-grid FU coordinate are intentionally not reverse-buildable without raw FU; likewise `Outside 50` lacks a side and is ambiguous. The Advanced raw-FU escape hatch preserves those exact legacy/debug cases. Future performer instructions should use builder-valid quarter-step notation or include an explicit raw/side disambiguator where exact off-grid placement is required.
