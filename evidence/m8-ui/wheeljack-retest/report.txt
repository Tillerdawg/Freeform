F2 — redraw destroys focus and live-node identity

  (host.replaceChildren(), not host.replaceWith()); redraw() captures the focused control's id
  before rebuilding and restores it afterward (falling back to #export-panel-heading when open,

  Evidence: export-ui.test.ts "interpolates count failures..." asserts focus remains on the radio

  focus after its own change event in the actual app, and "director-ready-focus" confirms the
  Download button receives focus once ready while the submit button was focused at submission.

F3 — range controls do not refresh transition eligibility
  Fixed: the "From set"/"To set" <select> change handlers now call redraw() immediately, so the
  transition fieldset re-renders against the new range on every change, not only on an unrelated
  rebuild. normalizedRequest() independently validates every checked transition id against the
  live document and the current scope (transitionInScope()), returning the approved
  transitionOutsideRange / unknownTransition messageKey instead of silently iterating only
  eligibleTransitions() and dropping a stale checked id.
  Evidence: export-ui.test.ts "refreshes range eligibility and rejects a retained checked
  transition instead of silently dropping it" — narrows a 3-set range so a previously checked
  transition's row disappears, then asserts submit produces the exact approved error text instead
  of a silently narrowed request.

  line/detail disclosure; `outcome` (the ready bytes/filename/warnings) is left untouched, so the
  Download button and its href/bytes survive a failed dispatch and a second click retries the
  exact same bytes without re-invoking the writer.
  Evidence: export-ui.test.ts "keeps ready bytes and a retry control after a simulated
